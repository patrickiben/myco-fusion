natural_objects_27band.csv is derived from:

Gutiérrez, A. et al. Spectral dataset of natural objects' reflectance from the Southern
cone of South America. Scientific Data 12, 594 (2025). Dataset on Figshare:
https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.25705380.v4. License: CC BY 4.0.

Changes: repeat measurements averaged per sample, interpolated to 420-680 nm in 10 nm
steps, clipped to [0, 1], and one color label's spelling corrected.
